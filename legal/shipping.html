Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

OMNIPOTENT sells digital products and services only. Nothing bought here is physically shipped — everything is delivered instantly to your account, with no courier, no tracking number and no delivery charge. This policy explains how and when what you buy reaches you.

1. What we deliver

  • Memberships (Basic, OMNI Pro, and coached tiers) — access to the OMNI AI coach, your personalised training and nutrition plan, tracking features, and, on coached tiers, a dedicated human coach.
  • Programs (such as the OMNIPOTENT Signature Program) — structured training content unlocked inside your account.

All of the above are delivered electronically. There is no physical shipment, no courier, no tracking number and no delivery charge.

2. When delivery happens

Access is granted immediately and automatically to the account used at checkout as soon as your payment is confirmed by our payment provider — normally within a few seconds. You do not need to wait for an email or take any further action; simply open the app and your purchase is active.

In the rare case that a payment is held for verification by the bank or payment provider, access is granted as soon as that confirmation is received, typically within 24 hours.

3. How you receive it

Your purchase is delivered inside the OMNIPOTENT app at app.omnipotentdev.com, which you can use in any modern browser or install to your home screen. A confirmation of your order is also sent to the email address used at checkout.

4. Delivery period of your membership

A membership gives access for the billing period you chose — monthly, 3 months or a year. Coached tiers also include your human coach's ongoing service for that period.

Most memberships are auto-renewing subscriptions: access continues, and the same amount is charged again at the end of each period, until you cancel in the app. A few high-value plans and the Signature Program are one-time purchases — access simply ends at the close of the period and you are never charged again. Full detail is in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

5. If you don't receive access

If your payment was successful but your account has not unlocked, email omnipotent.coach@gmail.com from the email address used at checkout, with your order number. We aim to respond within 1 business day and will either restore your access or, if we cannot, refund the payment in full.

The most common cause is paying with a different email address than the one on your OMNIPOTENT account. Tell us both addresses and we will link them for you.

6. International customers

Because delivery of our memberships and programs is entirely digital, the service is available wherever you can access the internet, subject to our Terms of Service. No customs, duties or import charges apply to digital purchases.

7. Products sold on our store

Any physical products are sold separately on our online store, not through this app. Where we link to that store, those purchases — including their shipping, delivery and returns — are governed by the policies published on the store at the point of sale.
