Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OMNIPOTENT ("OMNIPOTENT", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the OMNIPOTENT app and website (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address (and display name if you sign in with Google) used to create and secure your account.
  • Fitness & profile data you provide — name, age, sex, height, weight, training experience, goals, dietary preferences, training schedule, injuries, and the information you enter during onboarding and while using the app.
  • Activity you log — workouts, nutrition entries, body metrics, and your conversations with OMNI (the AI coach).
  • Subscription & billing records — your plan, its status (active, cancelled, expired), your billing period and renewal dates, the amounts charged, and the reference IDs our payment provider gives each payment. We do not store your card number, CVV, or UPI PIN.
  • Technical data — basic device and usage information needed to run the Service securely (for example, request timing for rate limiting).

2. How we use your information

  • To create your personalised training and nutrition plan and operate the app's features.
  • To power OMNI, the AI coach, so it can respond with context about your goals and logs.
  • To save your data so it syncs across your devices and is restored if you reinstall.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and provide customer support.

3. Service providers we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data with trusted providers only to run the Service:

  • Google Firebase — for sign-in and account authentication, and to store the workouts, meals, body weight and other logs you enter in your personal training tracker.
  • Anthropic — your messages to OMNI (and relevant context) are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to generate responses.
  • Render — our hosting and database provider, where your data is stored.
  • Razorpay — our payment provider. When you buy a membership or program, Razorpay processes the payment, and for auto-renewing subscriptions it also securely stores the UPI Autopay or card mandate used to take each renewal. Your card details and UPI credentials are entered on Razorpay's secure checkout — we never see or store your full card number, CVV, or UPI PIN.

4. Data storage & security

Your data is stored on secured servers and protected by authentication so that only your logged-in account can access it. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction — you can view and edit your profile and logs in the app at any time.
  • Deletion — you can permanently delete your account and associated data from inside the app (Profile → Delete account), or by emailing us. This removes your account, profile, logs, and synced data.

If you are in India, you have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may contact us to exercise these rights.

6. Children

The Service is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
