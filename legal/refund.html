Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

This policy explains how billing, cancellation, and refunds work for OMNIPOTENT memberships and programs (the "Service"). By buying from us you agree to this policy. Payments are processed by our payment provider, Razorpay.

The short version. Most memberships renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel yourself in the app, at any time, in a few taps — no email, no phone call, nothing to sit through. Cancelling stops the next charge, and you keep your access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

1. Two ways you can buy

Auto-renewing subscriptions. Most memberships are subscriptions. You authorise a UPI Autopay or card mandate once at checkout, and the same amount is then charged automatically at the end of every billing period — monthly, every 3 months, or yearly, depending on the plan you picked — until you cancel.

One-time purchases. These are paid once, give a fixed period of access, and never charge you again. They are:

  • OMNI + Coach — Annual (₹44,999)
  • OMNI 24/7 Coach — 3 Months (₹25,649)
  • OMNI 24/7 Coach — Annual (₹85,499)
  • The OMNIPOTENT Signature Program (₹899)

Those three memberships are sold as one-time purchases because RBI's e-mandate rules require you to individually approve every automatic debit above ₹15,000. Rather than make you approve a notification each time, we simply charge once for the whole period. When it ends, access stops and buying again is your choice.

The app tells you which of the two you are buying before you pay.

2. How auto-renewal works

  • The price shown in the app is the price charged each period. No taxes or fees are added at checkout.
  • Your bank or UPI app sends you a pre-debit notification before each automatic charge, so a renewal is never a surprise.
  • Renewals continue until you cancel. There is no minimum number of periods and no lock-in.
  • If an automatic payment fails, Razorpay retries it over the following days. If every retry fails, the subscription halts and your access ends at the close of the period you have already paid for.

3. Cancelling — how, and what happens

You can cancel a subscription yourself, at any time, from inside the app: open Profile → Membership and cancel there. The cancellation registers immediately. You do not need to email us, wait for a reply, or turn down a retention offer.

When you cancel:

  • No further payments are taken — the mandate stops.
  • You keep your access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling does not cut you off on the spot.
  • Cancelling is not backdated. It stops future renewals; on its own it is not a refund of the period you are currently in. Sections 5 and 6 cover when we do refund.
  • You can subscribe again later whenever you want.

One-time purchases (Section 1) have nothing to cancel — they never renew.

4. Refunds — the general position

Memberships and programs are digital and are delivered instantly: the moment your payment is confirmed, your account unlocks. Because of that, payments are non-refundable once access has been delivered, except in the cases set out below. Simply not using what you bought is not on its own a reason for a refund.

5. Exception — a renewal you did not mean to keep

If an auto-renewal is charged and you did not intend to continue, contact us within 7 days of that charge. If you have not meaningfully used the Service in that new period, we will review it and can refund that renewal and cancel the subscription for you. This covers the renewal charge only, not earlier periods you have already used.

6. Exception — coached service failure

Coached memberships (OMNI + Coach and OMNI 24/7 Coach) include a dedicated human coach. If your assigned coach fails to provide the service as described — for example, does not respond or does not deliver your plan within a reasonable time — or behaves unprofessionally, contact us within your active membership period. We will review the case and, at our reasonable discretion, may reassign your coach or issue a partial or full refund for the affected period.

7. The Signature Program (₹899)

The OMNIPOTENT Signature Program is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The full program unlocks in your account immediately on payment, so it is non-refundable once delivered. If it does not unlock, or you were charged for it more than once, tell us — we will either fix the access or refund you in full.

8. How to ask for a refund

Email achievingomnipotence@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with your payment or order number and a short description of what happened. We aim to respond within 3 business days.

You never need to email us just to cancel — cancelling is self-service in the app (Section 3). Email is for refund requests and problems.

9. Approved refunds

Where a refund is approved, it is processed to your original payment method via Razorpay. Refunds typically take 5–7 business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer. Access bought with the refunded payment ends when the refund is issued.
